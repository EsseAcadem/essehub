Spiritual Care

Our multi-faith chaplaincy team provides spiritual, pastoral and religious care to patients, relatives and carers, as well as staff and visitors.

The team supports people of all faiths and beliefs, including people with no particular religious belief.

!Emergency referral

  • Call the switchboard and ask for the on-call chaplain or faith leader.
  • A chaplain will usually attend within 1 hour.
  • If a specific faith leader is needed, this can take up to 6 hours.

○Non-emergency referral

Call the relevant chaplaincy office. A chaplain will usually attend within 24 hours.

Employee Assistance Programme

The Employee Assistance Programme provides free and confidential wellbeing support for all staff, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

You can self-refer for support with issues affecting your wellbeing at work or at home. The Trust and your manager will not be informed if you contact the service.

✓Support available

  • Immediate telephone support and advice.
  • Up to 6 structured counselling sessions, online, by phone or in person.
  • Support with stress, relationships, family, debt, money and workplace issues.
  • Emotional, financial and work-related advice.
  • Some language support and translation services are available on request.

☎Contact the EAP

0800 023 9324 0330 380 2056

Available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

The service is confidential. Your manager and the Trust will not be told that you have used it.
